    Calculator help & keyboard shortcuts

    Tap the keys to enter a calculation, then = to calculate. Use DEG or RAD for trigonometry, and Ans to reuse the last answer. Inverse trigonometry, powers, roots, logarithms and factorials have their own keys. Tap an expression or answer in the display to recall it. An operator after a calculation continues with Ans; (−) enters a negative number.

    On a keyboard, Enter calculates, Backspace deletes and Escape clears the entry. Arrow keys move through the entry or calculation history. Copy copies the latest result; Clear removes saved entries. Calculations and angle mode are saved in this browser. Use Pop out for a separate calculator window. Choose a calculator from the menu beside the Gregle logo.

    Touch keys provide a light vibration when supported by your phone and browser.
