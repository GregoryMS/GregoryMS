    Keyboard shortcuts & a few useful tips

    Use your keyboard or the number pad. Enter or = calculates, Backspace deletes, and Esc clears. You can select and edit any part of a calculation.

    Brackets and the usual order of operations are supported: 2 + 3 × 4 = 14. Powers come before a leading minus: −2^2 = −4. Use (−2)^2 for 4.

    For a percentage, 200 + 10% = 220, 200 − 10% = 180 and 200 × 10% = 20. A percentage on its own divides by 100.

    Scientific mode adds powers, logarithms, factorials and trigonometry. Choose DEG for degrees or RAD for radians. Close function brackets, for example sin(30). Ans uses the last calculated answer from this visit.

    Results are rounded to 14 significant digits for display. Calculations and history stay on your device.
