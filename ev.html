How the estimate is calculated
Units are converted to km and kWh per 100 km first. Driving energy = distance in km × kWh per 100 km ÷ 100. Energy purchased = driving energy ÷ (charging efficiency ÷ 100). Trip cost = energy purchased × electricity price per kWh. A return trip doubles the distance.
