    How to read the observing outlook

    “Promising” means astronomical darkness (Sun below −18°), cloud cover at most 25%, rain probability below 20% and wind at most 15 km/h. “Poor” means clouds above 60%, rain probability at least 50% or wind above 30 km/h; other dark hours are “Mixed”. This is a simple planning guide, not a seeing or transparency forecast. Check the Moon and the Clear Sky Chart below. Dew risk is high within 2°C of the dew point and moderate within 4°C. “—” and “Unknown” mean missing data.

    Chart key · colours & rows

    Read down a column for the same forecast time. Times, time-zone offset and issue date are printed on the chart. Scroll the chart sideways on mobile.

    Cloud cover
    Clear→OvercastDeeper blue means fewer clouds. Separate cloud rows compare forecast models.
    Transparency & seeing
    Poor→BetterTransparency: clarity for faint objects. Seeing: steady air for sharp detail. White means too cloudy to forecast.
    Darkness
    Dark sky→DaylightIncludes sunlight and moonlight, but not local light pollution.
    Smoke
    Little / none→More smokeColours move through pale shades and red to brown as smoke increases.
    Wind & humidity
    Wind: dark blue is calmer; pale shades are windier. Humidity: blue is drier; orange/red is more humid, with greater dew risk.
    Temperature
    Blue/cyan is colder; yellow, orange and red are warmer. Check the provider’s full legend for exact ranges and units.

        Upcoming satellite passes ↗Satellite details ↗

        Positions are calculated from orbit data and update every 10 seconds while visible. The dashed orange line projects the next 90 minutes; the solid violet line builds a recent track as you watch. These are estimates, not live telemetry. Orbit data refreshes every six hours; older data is labelled, and data over seven days old is not used.

        Elevation is the angle above your selected location’s horizon, assuming sea level. A satellite above the horizon may still be invisible: check pass predictions, local darkness and clouds before heading outside.

        Orbit data: CelesTrak · Calculations: Satellite.js · Pass predictions: Heavens-Above. Pass links default to UTC; check the time zone shown on the pass page. Opening pass predictions shares the selected observing coordinates with Heavens-Above.
