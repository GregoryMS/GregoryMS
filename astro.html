      How to read the observing outlook

      “Promising” means astronomical darkness (Sun below −18°), cloud cover at most 25%, rain probability below 20% and wind at most 15 km/h. “Poor” means clouds above 60%, rain probability at least 50% or wind above 30 km/h; other dark hours are “Mixed”. This is a simple planning guide, not a seeing or transparency forecast. Check the Moon and the Clear Sky Chart above. Dew risk is high within 2°C of the dew point and moderate within 4°C. “—” and “Unknown” mean missing data.

      Sky charts: Clear Sky Chart · Environment Canada regional charts · Conditions & hourly forecast: Open-Meteo · Search: GeoNames via Open-Meteo · Phase dates: US Naval Observatory · Sun & Moon: SunCalc.

      Current conditions are modelled estimates. Forecasts refresh every 15 minutes while visible. Location searches go to Open-Meteo; selected coordinates go to the forecast and map providers. My location asks permission and rounds coordinates to two decimals. Locations are not saved.
