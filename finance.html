About data & favourites

Charts and quotes are supplied by TradingView. Availability and delays depend on the exchange and data feed. Check each widget’s timestamps and market status; a closed market can show its last traded price. Index CFDs and ETFs are labelled separately from indices. Favourites are saved only in this browser. Quick-search suggestions cover a small built-in directory; the chart’s own symbol search covers TradingView’s available listings. An internet connection and access to TradingView are required.
