About your reader

Import an OPML file from another reader to bring your feeds and folders with you. RSS, Atom and JSON Feed are supported. Open a story for its feed summary or visit the original article. Some publishers provide only a short excerpt.

Feeds load directly when possible. If a publisher blocks browser access, its public feed URL is sent to rss2json or AllOrigins to retrieve it. These services can be unavailable or return limited results. Use public feeds only; URLs containing passwords or access tokens should not be added. Subscriptions, cached stories, bookmarks and read status stay in this browser and do not sync across devices. Export OPML backs up subscriptions.
