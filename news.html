About your reader

BBC News, SVT Nyheter, NRK, Reuters, CBC News and DW Deutsch are included by default. SVT stays in Swedish, NRK in Norwegian and DW in German. Reuters headlines come through a Reuters-only Google News feed. CBC uses its own feed, with a CBC-only Google News fallback if the publisher cannot be reached. Remove any source in Manage, or add your own.

Import an OPML file from another reader to bring your feeds and folders with you. RSS, Atom and JSON Feed are supported. Open a story for its feed summary or visit the original article. Some publishers provide only a short excerpt.

The default feeds load from Gregle, with updates scheduled every 15 minutes. If a copy is more than two hours old, the reader tries the publisher directly, then rss2json or AllOrigins. Added feeds use those direct and proxy routes too. These services can be unavailable or return limited results. Use public feeds only; URLs containing passwords or access tokens should not be added. Subscriptions, cached stories, bookmarks and read status stay in this browser and do not sync across devices. Export OPML backs up subscriptions.
