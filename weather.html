About this map

Environment Canada’s surface precipitation-type composite combines radar observations and modelled surface temperatures to estimate what is reaching the ground. It updates every six minutes at 1 km resolution, with up to three hours of history. This is a recent estimate, not a forecast. Coverage varies across North America; blank areas may mean no precipitation or missing radar coverage. Times use the selected location’s time zone. Map areas are requested from OpenStreetMap and Environment Canada. About the data ↗

About this map

Forecast ground-level wildfire smoke, shown every three hours. This is a model estimate of smoke PM₂.₅, not measured air quality or AQHI. Clear areas may have less than 1 µg/m³ of modelled smoke; forecasts can miss fires and local conditions. Coverage is limited to the outlined forecast area. Gregle creates this map from BlueSky Canada’s raw forecast data, checked hourly for updates. Times use the selected location’s time zone. About the data ↗
