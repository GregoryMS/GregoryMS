About this map

Wind and cloud forecasts are provided by Windy using ECMWF. The Clouds view also shows forecast rain. Use the map’s colour key and timeline to explore the forecast. Radar shows past precipitation observations. Windy receives the selected map coordinates when you open a forecast layer. The embedded map has its own pan, zoom and forecast controls; Recenter returns to your selected city.
