# Calculator icon

Original vector artwork for Gregle. Charcoal `#232323`, off-white `#faf9f6` and violet `#9850ff` match the existing app family. The calculator silhouette and purple display/equal key remain legible at launcher size.

`icon.svg` is the master. `python3 scripts/render_calc_icons.py` reproduces the opaque 180px Apple touch icon, 192px/512px app icons, 32px favicon and multi-size ICO with supersampling. The operating system applies its Home Screen mask.
