# Station artwork

These are broadcaster logos used to identify the stations, not Gregle branding. Rights remain with their owners.

- `bbc-radio-1.png`: official BBC Radio 1 600 × 600 PNG, unchanged, from https://sounds.files.bbci.co.uk/3.12.0/networks/bbc_radio_one/blocks-colour_600x600.png (linked by the BBC Radio 1 live page).
- `nrk-p3.svg`: official NRK P3 wordmark, unchanged, from https://lyd.nrk.no/img/logos/p3.svg (NRK's official stream directory).
- `nrk-p3.png`: the same NRK SVG rasterized to a 512 × 512 square for Media Session artwork. The wordmark keeps its original proportions and colours, centred at 384px wide on a #121212 background so its white lettering remains visible.

Retrieved 23 September 2026. PNG versions are used both on the page and in system media controls.
